Guest Presence System

Know when a room is occupied — without disturbing the guest

Know whether a guest is in the room — clearly and safely

The EMROS Guest Presence System helps hotel staff know whether a room is occupied or vacant, without knocking or disturbing the guest.

Using discreet motion sensors and door sensors, the system automatically updates room status, improving staff awareness while maintaining guest privacy.

This helps hotels to:

  • Know whether a guest is inside the room
  • Avoid entering rooms when guests are present
  • Approach rooms at the right time
  • Improve staff awareness without disturbing guests
