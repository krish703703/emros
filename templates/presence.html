Guest Presence System

Know if a Guest Is in the Room — Without Disturbing Them

Accurate Presence Detection

The EMROS Guest Presence System determines room occupancy using intelligent wireless motion sensors and door contacts.

How It Works

  • Motion sensors scan for presence after the door closes
  • Movement marks the room as "Occupied"
  • Status updates whenever the door opens or closes

This data is shared with hotel staff or integrated with the PMS, ensuring housekeeping never enters an occupied room.
