What Is EMROS?

We make hotel rooms smarter — without changing how guests relax.

EMROS is a hotel technology company that helps hotels run guest rooms in a smarter, quieter, and more efficient way. Our systems take care of everyday room functions such as lighting, temperature, privacy settings, and energy usage — all automatically.

Guests don’t need to learn anything new or press extra buttons. Everything works naturally in the background. This means guests enjoy a comfortable and peaceful stay, hotel staff always know what is happening in each room, and hotel management gains better control over operations and costs.

With EMROS, hotels can:

  • Protect guest privacy automatically and avoid unnecessary interruptions
  • Help housekeeping and maintenance teams know what each room needs, in real time
  • Reduce electricity and energy waste when rooms are not occupied
  • Deliver a smooth, comfortable, and consistent experience for every guest
