Guest Room Management System

A room that adjusts automatically to your guest experience

Customize how guests experience the room

The EMROS Guest Room Management System helps hotels define how they want the guest experience to feel. Hotels can set simple rules that decide what happens when a guest enters the room, stays inside, or leaves.

For example, when a guest enters, lighting can switch on and the air-conditioning can adjust to a preset comfort level. When the room is not in use, settings return to the hotel’s preferred state — all automatically and without guest effort.

With GRMS, hotels can:

  • Customize room behaviour to match their brand experience
  • Set automatic actions when a guest enters or leaves
  • Control lighting, temperature, curtains, and room scenes
  • Deliver a consistent and comfortable stay for every guest
