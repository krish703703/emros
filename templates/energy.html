Energy Management System

Smart Savings Without Compromising Guest Comfort

The Challenge

Hotels face rising energy costs and the constant struggle of balancing efficiency with guest satisfaction. Cooling and heating often account for 45% of total energy usage.

The EMROS Solution

Instead of switching everything off, EMROS intelligently adjusts temperature and lighting when rooms are unoccupied. As soon as a guest returns, the room instantly restores their preferred settings.

  • Significantly lower energy bills
  • Zero impact on guest comfort
  • Intelligent PMS integration
